Shuttle Camera 0.1.31

A simpler interface for capture and HDMI output, with a compact layout, short labels and a new original SVHS icon.

Diagnostics and driver setup are now under Help. About and Quit are in the Shuttle Camera menu.

Quit Premiere and Final Cut, then stop output and quit Shuttle Camera before installing. Enter your administrator password when asked. Existing settings are retained.

The capture, HDMI output and audio drivers, USB playback engine and Premiere transmitter are unchanged. No driver reactivation is needed.
