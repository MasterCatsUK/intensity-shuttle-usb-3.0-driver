# Startup movie

Startup.m4v was supplied by Andrew for the Shuttle Video project on23October2026 and inclusion in its public release. It is a5.64-second1920×1080/50fps export with the Shuttle Video logo as its final picture. Retain attribution to the project/Andrew when redistributing. The app stretches it to the selected output raster, plays once and holds the last frame silently until an app action. No other media or Final Cut library is included.
